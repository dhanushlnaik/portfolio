## Why I built it

There are a lot of stray animals where I live, and the people who rescue or feed them mostly coordinate over WhatsApp groups. Adoption drives happen, but there wasn't one simple place to post "I found this dog, it needs a home" and have someone nearby see it.

So I built **StrayMate**.

## Stack

- **Next.js** for frontend and backend
- **TypeScript**
- **Prisma + PostgreSQL** for the database
- **NextAuth.js** for sign-in
- **Tailwind CSS** for styling

## What was hard

Building the screens was quick. Making them easy for people who aren't very online took longer. I cut a lot of fields from the listing form after watching friends try to use it, because every extra field meant fewer people finished posting.

## What I took from it

I care more about whether a project gets used than how impressive the code is. StrayMate was small, but it was the first thing I built that I could imagine helping someone I'd never meet.
