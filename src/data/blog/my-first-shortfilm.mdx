## The idea

It started with one question: what if a student met his alter ego, the version of himself he wished he was?

That turned into a short film about identity, insecurity and fitting in.

## The story

The alter ego is confident and popular, everything the main character isn't. The twist is that they're the same person. I wanted it to say something about how college pushes everyone to become the same kind of student.

## Making it

Shooting was harder than I expected. Lighting, sound and keeping the mood right all took more takes than planned. I edited everything in **Adobe Premiere Pro** and graded it so the colours get colder as the character falls apart.

## What I learned

Pacing matters in film the same way it matters in a UI. If a scene or a screen holds people too long, they drift off. That's carried over into how I design and edit for clients now.
