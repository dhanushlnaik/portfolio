## The Concept

The story began as a random thought —  
"What if a student met his alter ego, who represents everything he wanted to be?"

It evolved into a **visually philosophical short film** about identity, insecurity, and conformity.

---

## The Theme

The alter ego is confident, popular, and everything the protagonist is not.  
But the twist — they’re the same person.  
It’s a reflection of how colleges often produce “factory students” chasing validation.

---

## The Process

Filming it wasn’t easy — managing lighting, sound, and editing while keeping the tone *haunting yet human*.  
I edited it all in **Adobe Premiere Pro**, with color grading and cuts that mirror the protagonist’s emotional descent.

---

## The Takeaway

It reminded me that **storytelling and technology aren’t separate**.  
Whether it’s code or cinema — both need empathy, clarity, and rhythm.
