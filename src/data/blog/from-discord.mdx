## The Beginning

I wrote my first few lines of Python back in 10th grade.  
I didn’t know it then, but that small curiosity slowly became the foundation for everything I do now.

Later, I explored **Android Studio with Java**, building small apps just to see how “real software” worked.  

But my real turning point came when I became an **admin of a massive Discord server** — over **45,000 members**, belonging to a **YouTuber with 4M+ subscribers**.

---

## Learning by Doing

Running that server taught me more than I expected —  
how communities behave, how systems scale, and how to handle chaos (especially when 10,000 people ping a bot at once 😭).

So I co-developed a **verified gaming bot called “Not Amy”**, collaborating with other developers.  
That project taught me backend logic, APIs, rate limiting, and async handling before I even knew what those words meant.

---

## From Curiosity to Creation

That experience slowly shifted my focus from just “tech for fun” to **building things with purpose**.  
I started exploring **web development**, **JavaScript**, and eventually found love in **Next.js**, **TypeScript**, and **Node.js**.

I didn’t plan to become a full-stack developer — it just happened by constantly asking:  
> “Can I make this better?”

And that question still drives me every day.
