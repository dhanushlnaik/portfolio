## Overview

**ClockThatDaily** is a daily puzzle in the spirit of the NYT games, but about the internet. Each day a new set of questions lands, and you get about four minutes to prove you were paying attention to cinema, gaming, anime, music and brainrot lore.

## The four tracks

- **Emojidle**: decode a movie, show, game or song from five emoji clues, with three guesses.
- **Bad Plot**: name the work from a deliberately terrible plot summary.
- **Soundbite**: identify the clip.
- **Cluster**: group the connected items.

## The loop

You play, earn **Aura**, keep your **streak** alive and climb the **global leaderboard**. The design leans into a printed-catalogue look with numbered "artifacts" and a daily press date, so each day feels like a new issue.

## Built with

Next.js and TypeScript, deployed on Vercel. The source is private for now.
