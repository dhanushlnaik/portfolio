## Overview

**DeliverZip** was created for a hackathon to solve regional delivery issues by leveraging existing bus and train routes. The platform lets users book, schedule, and track deliveries affordably and efficiently.

## Key Features

- **Booking Interface** — Create delivery requests using nearby transport routes.  
- **Mock Schedule System** — Simulates delivery tracking.  
- **Notifications** — Email or dashboard-based delivery updates.  
- **Regional Impact** — Focused on sustainable logistics for rural areas.
