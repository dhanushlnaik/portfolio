## Overview

**DeliverZip** was a 24-hour hackathon project. The idea: small towns already have buses and trains running between them every day, so why not send parcels on them? You book a delivery, pick a route, and track it.

## Key Features

- **Booking**: create a delivery on a nearby bus or train route.  
- **Mock schedules**: fake timetable data to simulate tracking.  
- **Updates**: delivery status by email or on the dashboard.  
- **Who it's for**: towns that big courier networks don't cover well.
