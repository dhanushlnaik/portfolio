## Overview

**EasyFarm** helps farmers track their yield, connect with others, and access local agricultural insights. Designed with simplicity in mind, the platform promotes tech adoption in rural areas.

## Key Features

- **Yield Dashboard** — Track data trends.  
- **Community Portal** — Connect with other farmers.  
- **Responsive Design** — Works across devices for rural accessibility.
