## Overview

I was lead developer on the **CSI NMAMIT website**: events, memberships and the chapter's blog. The main goal was making it easy for next year's team to update content without touching code.

## Key Features

- **Events & blog**: generated from structured data files.  
- **Mobile-first**: most students open it on their phones.  
- **Handover**: small, reusable components so juniors can pick it up.
