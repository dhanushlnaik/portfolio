## Overview

**Not Amy** is a verified Discord bot developed for a large gaming community. It automates moderation, manages events, and integrates game-based features.

- **Gaming Integrations** — XP, leaderboard, and fun commands.  
- **Verified Bot** — Managed across a 45K+ member server.
